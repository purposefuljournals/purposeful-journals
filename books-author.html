Meet the Author

Words That Connect. Stories That Stay.

Ceblle Dawson is an author whose writing explores love, relationships, emotional connection and the complexities of finding oneself.

Through relatable characters and heartfelt storytelling, she explores the experiences that shape us, the relationships that challenge us and the courage it takes to choose ourselves.

Her work is published under the Purposeful Journals identity, rooted in the belief that purpose should always come before pressure.

Featured Novel

The Love You Don't Have to Beg For

A contemporary romance novel by Ceblle Dawson

She knew what it felt like to be wanted. She was learning what it meant to be loved.

Danielle Hartley thought she had outgrown the kind of love that left her waiting for a message, replaying conversations and turning promises into evidence that she mattered.

Then she met Tariq. But as the difference between chemistry and commitment becomes impossible to ignore, Danielle must confront how much of herself she is willing to sacrifice to be chosen.

A moving story about desire, disappointment, self-worth and the courage to recognise that love should never require you to abandon yourself.
